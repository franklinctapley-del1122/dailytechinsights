Semrush vs Ahrefs

A practical comparison of two major SEO platforms, focused on keyword research, competitor analysis, technical SEO, backlinks, rank tracking, content workflows and AI-search visibility.

Updated September 18, 2026 · By DailyTechInsights Editorial Team
Quick answer: Semrush and Ahrefs both cover core SEO research, but their current product ecosystems and workflows differ. Semrush emphasizes a broad SEO and marketing toolkit that includes keyword research, competitor analysis, technical site audit, rank tracking, backlink analysis, content and AI-visibility workflows. Ahrefs combines Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer, Site Audit, Rank Tracker, Content Explorer and Brand Radar around search, web and AI-visibility research. The right fit depends on the tasks, data, integrations and reporting process your team actually uses.
How to use this comparison: Feature counts alone are not enough. Run the same representative workflow in each product—such as researching a topic, auditing a site, analyzing competitors, tracking rankings and producing a report—before deciding.

1. Keyword Research

Semrush's current keyword research materials cover keyword discovery, search metrics, keyword gaps, competitor terms and opportunities to optimize existing content.

Ahrefs' Keywords Explorer is designed for keyword research and provides keyword metrics and ranking-context data; Ahrefs also connects keyword discovery with Site Explorer and Rank Tracker workflows.

The practical test is not which database sounds larger. Check whether each platform returns useful terms for your market, location and search intent, and whether you can move from discovery into the next action without excessive manual work.

2. Competitor and Site Research

Semrush positions competitor analysis around organic rankings, keyword gaps, traffic/market research and related visibility signals. Its SEO toolkit also provides competitor-facing research through project and domain workflows.

Ahrefs Site Explorer emphasizes competitor organic and AI-search traffic, top pages, keywords, backlinks and paid traffic, allowing users to study how a domain is discovered and which pages drive visibility.

3. Technical SEO

Semrush Site Audit is positioned around crawling websites to identify technical issues such as broken links, crawlability blockers and other SEO problems.

Ahrefs also includes Site Audit as a core tool and describes it as a crawler for technical and on-page issues.

For a real selection decision, compare crawl configuration, issue prioritization, historical reporting, segmentation and how comfortably your team turns findings into tickets or fixes.

4. Backlink Analysis

Both platforms provide backlink research. Semrush includes backlink analysis and competitor link-gap workflows; Ahrefs uses Site Explorer and its backlink index to study referring pages, competitors and link profiles.

When evaluating link data, focus on the domains and page types you actually work with, how quickly new links appear, filtering, historical access and exports. A large index only matters when its coverage is useful for your market.

5. Rank Tracking and Reporting

Semrush documents daily rank tracking across locations, devices and search platforms, plus competitor monitoring and reporting.

Ahrefs provides Rank Tracker for ongoing keyword monitoring and separates that workflow from the automatically populated ranking data in Site Explorer.

6. AI-Search Visibility

Both companies now describe AI visibility as part of their products, although terminology and measurement methods differ. Semrush highlights visibility across AI systems and AI Overviews, while Ahrefs describes Brand Radar and AI-search visibility through its current platform.

Treat these metrics as vendor-specific measurement systems rather than interchangeable search rankings. Compare the models, tracked prompts, reporting and historical consistency before using them for internal KPIs.

7. Pricing and Total Cost

Pricing and feature packaging change regularly, and both vendors package capabilities, limits and add-ons in ways that make a simple headline-price comparison misleading. Review the current official pricing and plan documentation at the time of purchase, then model the cost for your actual seats, projects, tracked keywords, crawl needs and reporting requirements.

Are Semrush and Ahrefs the same type of software?

They overlap heavily in SEO research, but their product packaging and broader workflow coverage differ. Compare the exact tasks and limits your team needs.

Can I use both Semrush and Ahrefs?

Yes. Some teams use more than one SEO platform when different datasets or workflows justify the additional cost and operational complexity.
