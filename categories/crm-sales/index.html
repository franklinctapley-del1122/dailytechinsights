What Is CRM & Sales Software?

CRM and sales software supports the work of attracting prospects, managing customer information, progressing opportunities, coordinating sales activity and measuring outcomes. A CRM may also connect marketing, service, revenue and other customer-facing processes.

How to Evaluate CRM Software

  1. Define the sales or customer workflow.
  2. List required records, stages, fields and reports.
  3. Identify integrations and data flows.
  4. Separate must-have capabilities from nice-to-haves.
  5. Model total cost across seats, add-ons, onboarding and implementation.
  6. Run representative workflows before making a decision.
  7. Plan adoption, data governance and an exit path.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is CRM software?

CRM software helps organizations manage customer and prospect information, interactions, sales activity, workflows, reporting and related customer-facing processes.

What should I look for in CRM software?

Start with workflow fit, required data, usability, integrations, reporting, automation, security, scalability, total cost and the effort needed for implementation and adoption.

How do I compare CRM products?

Define the workflow first, then test the same realistic tasks in each candidate product and compare features, integrations, reporting, cost, governance and switching effort.
