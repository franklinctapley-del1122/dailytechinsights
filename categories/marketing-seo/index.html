How to Evaluate Marketing & SEO Software

  1. Define the marketing outcome before selecting a tool.
  2. Map the workflow from research to execution to measurement.
  3. Separate must-have data from useful extras.
  4. Check data freshness, geographic coverage and methodology.
  5. Test integrations with Google Search Console, analytics, CMS or reporting systems where relevant.
  6. Model total cost at the number of users, projects and tracked assets you actually need.
  7. Document what still requires manual work or another platform.
