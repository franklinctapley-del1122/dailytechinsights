Formula

Annual labor value = people × hours saved per week × hourly labor cost × 52.

Annual software cost = monthly subscription × 12.

Estimated ROI = (annual labor value − annual software cost) ÷ annual software cost × 100.

This simplified estimate is not a promise of savings. It excludes implementation, training, taxes, price changes, and adoption issues. Use realistic assumptions before making a purchasing decision.
