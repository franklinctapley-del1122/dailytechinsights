Slack Review

Slack is a team communication and work platform built around channels, messaging, huddles, canvases, lists, workflows, app integrations and AI-powered workplace intelligence.

Quick Verdict

Slack remains built around a simple idea: move work conversations out of scattered inboxes and into organized, searchable spaces. Channels, direct messages, huddles and shared canvases give teams multiple ways to communicate without leaving the same workspace.

What has changed is the scope. Slack now positions itself as a broader work operating system, adding Lists for task and project coordination, Workflow Builder for no-code automation, and AI features that summarize conversations, take huddle notes, search connected information and help users work with company knowledge.

Bottom line: Slack is strongest when a team has a high volume of ongoing collaboration and benefits from searchable shared context. The main consideration is governance: channel sprawl, notification volume and information overload can become real operational issues without clear team practices.

Slack Features

Slack's current feature set includes channels, messaging, Slack Connect, huddles, clips, canvases, lists, file sharing, templates, Workflow Builder and a large app marketplace. Slack says its marketplace has more than 2,600 apps and describes the product as a connected workplace across people, projects, knowledge and tools.

Slack AI & Workplace Intelligence

Slack's AI features include conversation summaries, huddle notes, search answers, recaps, file summaries, translations, AI workflow generation and Slackbot. Feature availability depends on the plan and workspace settings. Enterprise Search is positioned for organization-wide search across conversations and connected data.

Slack also describes an expanding agent platform, including Agentforce and custom-built AI assistants. Some agent capabilities are still described as coming soon, so buyers should distinguish available features from announced or preview functionality.

Ease of Use & User Experience

Slack is easy to understand at the basic level: teams create channels, send messages and use direct conversations. The complexity comes later, when organizations manage many channels, custom sections, external collaborators, workflows and integrations. Clear naming conventions, notification policies and channel ownership become more important as usage grows.

Integrations & Compatibility

Slack's integration ecosystem is one of its defining strengths. Slack says its marketplace includes more than 2,600 apps, including tools such as Salesforce, Jira, Google Drive and ChatGPT. The platform also provides APIs and an app platform for custom integrations and internal tools.

Who Should Use Slack?

Slack is a strong fit for: distributed teams, agencies, software companies, customer-facing organizations, cross-functional departments and businesses where fast collaboration and searchable conversation are central to how work gets done.

Who May Want an Alternative?

Teams that primarily need structured project scheduling, formal documentation or a highly centralized task system may want to pair Slack with a dedicated platform or evaluate a tool designed around that workflow.

Slack Alternatives

Microsoft Teams is a natural alternative for organizations deeply invested in Microsoft 365. Google Chat fits teams centered on Google Workspace. Discord can suit community-oriented communication. Rocket.Chat and other alternatives may be relevant where self-hosting or different governance requirements matter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Slack free?

Yes. Slack currently offers a Free plan at $0, with 90 days of searchable message history and limited app and meeting capabilities. The exact limits are documented on Slack's current pricing pages.

How much does Slack cost?

The current public pricing lists Pro at $7.25 per active user/month billed annually or $8.75 monthly, and Business+ at $15 annually or $18 monthly. Enterprise+ is custom-priced.

Does Slack have AI?

Yes. Slack offers AI summaries, huddle notes, search and recaps, and also promotes Slackbot and an expanding agent platform. AI availability varies by plan and workspace settings.

How many apps does Slack integrate with?

Slack says its App Marketplace offers more than 2,600 apps, alongside APIs and tools for custom integrations.

Is Slack good for project management?

Slack now includes canvases and lists for project coordination and task organization, but teams with deep scheduling or specialized project controls may still prefer a dedicated project-management platform.
