How We Review SaaS Software

A transparent framework for researching, testing, comparing and explaining software.

Our Review Process

01. Product & Market Research

We research product capabilities, target users, positioning, plans and use cases.

02. Hands-On Testing

Where practical, we evaluate onboarding, workflows, navigation and core features.

03. Feature Analysis

We assess the capabilities most relevant to the intended audience.

04. Pricing & Value

We examine plans, limits, add-ons and important buying considerations.

05. Competitors & Alternatives

We identify relevant competitors and meaningful product trade-offs.

06. Editorial Review & Updates

Reviews are checked for clarity, accuracy and material product changes.

Core Criteria

Features · Pricing · Ease of Use · Integrations · Business Fit · Alternatives
