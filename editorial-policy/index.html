DailyTechInsights Editorial Policy

Research & Sources

We prioritize primary sources such as official product documentation, pricing pages and release information for product facts. Where broader context is useful, we may consult reputable secondary sources.

Editorial Assessment

Our reviews distinguish product facts from DailyTechInsights analysis. Scores, where used, are editorial assessments rather than user-review averages.

Updates

We update pages when material product changes are identified, especially pricing, packaging, major features and availability.

Commercial Disclosure

Some pages may contain affiliate links or other commercial relationships. Where applicable, the relationship should be disclosed clearly near the relevant content.

Corrections

When a factual error is identified, we aim to correct it and update the page rather than silently preserving inaccurate information.
